WEEK 02 / EXERCISE 01

Your first HTML page

HTML gives content meaning and structure. A browser reads the document tree and uses elements such as headings and paragraphs to present a page.

LIVE EXAMPLE

Welcome to my page

This is a paragraph with important text and emphasis.

<!doctype html>
<html lang="en">
  <head>
    <meta charset="utf-8">
    <title>My first page</title>
  </head>
  <body>
    <h1>Welcome to my page</h1>
    <p>This is a paragraph with
      <strong>important text</strong>.</p>
  </body>
</html>
